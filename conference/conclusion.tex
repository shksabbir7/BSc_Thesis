\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This study shows that short Bangla cinema responses can be guided toward a human-recognizable engagement-specificity level while keeping the outcome verifier outside generation. Every registered alternative improved Verifier-B target probability over zero-shot, and the blinded readers usually recovered the requested level. The widening Verifier-A--Verifier-B gap during revision also shows that successful optimization against an in-loop score need not transfer at the same rate to a held-out instrument. Symbolic feedback remains useful as an interpretable diagnostic, but its advantage over neural-only gating is exploratory. Future work should test plot faithfulness, broaden the human evaluation, complete the separate English mirror, and examine whether the findings hold across other Bangla registers and generator families.
